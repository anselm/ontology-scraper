## Why this project exists

A discovery tool for organising the concept space of humanity. It reads the
news and recent essays on a schedule, finds the real things they are about
(a ship, a strait, a minister, a river, a storm), decides what kind of thing
each one is, and works out which reusable parts (components) those kinds are
built from. The parts are expected to be wrong at first; the tool exists to
refactor them over time as the material arrives. Anselm's brief, in his
words: "entity, prototype and component discovery, with refactoring over
time."

It continues `saltspring-ontology` (2026-08, one island's newspaper, ten
articles) at world scale, and takes its data model from the
`social-knowledge-service` design notes (2025-11): bare entities decorated
with normative components, prototypes instead of classes, relations as
entities with provenance.

Deployed to exe.dev; domain `ontology.monster` (Cloudflare, bought 2026-10).

## First milestones

1. Survey the literature and existing aggregators before writing code.
   (`20261014-survey.md`)
2. Architecture and module map; framing of users and screens.
   (`20261014-framing.md`, `20261014-architecture.md`)
3. Backend: sources → content → analysis → entities → prototypes →
   components as independent phases over mongo with dirty flags; a
   scheduler with backoff; socket.io transport.
4. Frontend: entities on a map with a time slider and a relationship graph;
   a view of prototypes and components; a notes page readers can follow.
5. Live on exe.dev, wired to ontology.monster.
