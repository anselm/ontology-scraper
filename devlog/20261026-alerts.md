# Status alerts over ntfy

2026-10-26. Anselm: "can you send periodic status alerts to nfty.sh/anselm-alerts - i didn't notice these
issues." Follows `20261026-starved-present.md`, which he found out about a week late. I read "nfty.sh" as
ntfy.sh, the push service; the topic is `anselm-alerts` as he wrote it.

## What runs

- `lib/alerts.mjs`: one POST per message to `$NTFY_URL/$NTFY_TOPIC`, no dependency; without a topic it logs
  and sends nothing, so a dev box never pages him.
- `lib/health.mjs` `check()`: a digest (fetched and read in 24 h, failures, budget used, spend today, backlog,
  age of the newest news read, counts, disk, the site's deployed sha) and a list of keyed problems. The
  problems are the failures this service has had: `stale` (newest news read is more than a day behind the
  newest fetched), `stalled` (no analysis for 3 h with work pending and budget left), `intake` (nothing fetched
  in 24 h), `billing` (402/429 errors in 24 h), `failures` (10+ and over 20% of the day's analyses), `disk`
  (over 85%), `site` (version.json not answering).
- `bin/health.mjs --notify`: pushes each problem once at high priority, again after 12 h if it persists, and
  an all-clear when it goes; and one digest a day at the first run after 16:00 UTC (morning in San Francisco;
  `DIGEST_HOUR`). Sent-state lives in the `state` collection under `health`. `--force` sends a digest now.
- `ontology-health.timer` on the VM runs it hourly (`deploy/provision.sh`, idempotent, re-run to install).
- The model layer pushes at once on a 402 from OpenRouter, since that is the failure that burned three
  attempts per document on 2026-10-19 and the hourly check would see it only afterwards.

## What it is not

Not a metrics system and not a watchdog on the VM itself: if the VM dies the timer dies with it, and nothing
external notices. A second check from canada1 (or the manager's audit) would cover that; not built.
